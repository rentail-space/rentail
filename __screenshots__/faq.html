What is specialty leasing?

Specialty leasing is a type of retail space that is leased for a short period of time, typically for a few days to a few months. It may be a kiosk, a cart, a booth, a pop-up shop, or a shared space.

What's the difference between a kiosk, cart, and pop-up shop?

Kiosks are semi-permanent structures (100-200 sq ft) in mall common areas. Carts are mobile retail units (40-80 sq ft) that can be moved. Pop-up shops are temporary storefronts (200-2000 sq ft) with full walls and doors. All three types are available on rentail.space across different shopping centers.
