How far in advance should I book?

Depends. Holiday season at a prime mall? Book months ahead. Off-season in a quieter location? You might snag same-day availability. Safe bet: 2-4 weeks out.

What if I have issues during my rental?

Contact the property manager. They'll sort it out.

It looks at what you're selling, your budget, who you're targeting, and foot traffic patterns. Then suggests spaces that actually make sense. And yeah, it gets better the more people use it.

What's the difference between a kiosk, cart, and pop-up shop?

Kiosks: semi-permanent structures in common areas (100-200 sq ft). Carts: mobile units you can wheel around (40-80 sq ft). Pop-ups: actual storefronts with walls and doors (200-2000 sq ft). We've got all three.

How much does it cost to rent a mall kiosk?

Varies wildly. Prime spots in major malls: $2,000-$8,000/month. Smaller markets: $500-$2,000/month. We show you the real price—no hidden fees tacked on.
