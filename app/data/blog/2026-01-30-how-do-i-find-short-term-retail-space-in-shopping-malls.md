## What Short-Term Leases Actually Look Like

Short-term retail leases range from one day to 24 months:

- **Pop-ups**: 4-6 weeks typical
- **Kiosks**: 3-12 months
- **Inline spaces**: Up to 24 months with favorable terms

Monthly costs: $1,500-$8,000+ for kiosks, $1,500-$10,000+ for pop-up spaces. Depends on mall traffic and location within the center.

Most short-term spaces come unfurnished. You provide fixtures, displays, and signage. Some landlords offer turnkey kiosks with built-in counters.

## Why Malls Offer Short-Term Leases

Malls have empty space. Short-term tenants fill it.

Seasonal traffic spikes need seasonal businesses. Landlords keep occupancy up, merchants test concepts without betting the farm.

Plus, rotating pop-ups create buzz. Fresh concepts draw visitors. That's why specialty leasing exists.
