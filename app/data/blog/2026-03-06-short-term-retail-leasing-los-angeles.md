## Cost Structure for Short-Term Retail Leases in LA

[Base rent in Los Angeles averages $38.30 per square foot annually](https://www.commercialcafe.com/retail/us/ca/los-angeles/). Pricing varies by location and property tier. Downtown LA retail space averages $19.77 per square foot. East Los Angeles runs around $25 per square foot.

Short-term leases use monthly rates instead of annual. You'll pay $4-10 per square foot monthly for most mall locations. Premium properties charge more.

Beyond base rent, budget for these additional costs:

- CAM (Common Area Maintenance) fees: $8-20 per square foot annually
- Utilities, which vary by space size and usage
- Marketing fund contributions (typically 1-2% of gross sales)
- Insurance requirements set by property management

Tenant improvement allowances for short-term leases are limited. Landlords can't amortize buildout costs over shorter periods. First-time tenants wanting 1-3 year terms should expect minimal TI investment from landlords.

Holiday season rates spike in November and December when foot traffic peaks. [The weeks before Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and Christmas bring massive shopper numbers](https://www.snipp.com/blog/2026-retail-seasonal-promotions-marketing-calendar). Premium locations cost more but generate higher sales.

## Lease Term Options and Flexibility

Shopping malls now offer flexible lease lengths beyond traditional long-term commitments. [Short-term leases run up to 24 months with favorable rent terms](https://capitalmallolympia.com/business/) for retailers testing markets or running seasonal campaigns.

Lease term preferences break down this way:

- 44% of tenants prefer three years or less
- 32% seek 3-5 year commitments
- 20% want leases beyond five years

National or regional retailers still need 5-10 year initial terms with renewal options. Smaller or emerging brands use specialty leasing programs for maximum flexibility.

[Pop-up shops at The Grove rotate every 8 weeks](https://www.brixmor.com/leasing/short-term-pop-up), giving brands quick market tests. Other malls offer daily rentals for events, week-long campaigns, or month-to-month kiosks and cart spots.

Shorter terms mean smaller tenant improvement budgets. Landlords need time to recoup buildout investments. If you need custom fixtures or extensive renovations, negotiate longer commitments or pay for improvements yourself.

[Brookfield Properties runs specialty leasing programs](https://brookfieldproperties.com/en/our-businesses/retail/leasing/kiosks-carts-specialty-leasing) for retail carts, kiosks, and pop-up shops. These programs help digital-first or emerging brands establish physical presence.

Some platforms offer [single-day to several-week rentals](https://www.thestorefront.com/search/los-angeles/retail). This makes it easy to align retail presence with specific events, product launches, or seasonal promotions.
