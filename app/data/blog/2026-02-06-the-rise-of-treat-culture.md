## The Problem: Big Goals Are Out of Reach

Traditional milestones—houses, cars, financial security—feel impossible for a generation facing stagnant wages and inflated costs.

So consumers redirect that spending power. Can't buy a house? At least you can reclaim agency with a fancy coffee. Can't save for retirement? At least you can afford that $15 candle.

**53% of young consumers** indulge in small treats because big milestones feel unreachable. This isn't impulse buying—it's emotional survival.
