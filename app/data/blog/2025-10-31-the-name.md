Finding short-term retail space used to mean calling dozens of brokers, navigating fragmented listings, hoping you'd stumble onto something. Rentail.space fixes that.

## The Problem: A Fragmented Market

The temporary retail space industry is fragmented as hell. Generic terms like "pop-up space" or "kiosk rental" get buried in search noise. Merchants waste weeks chasing leads. Shopping centers can't fill vacancies fast enough.

No central platform existed for short-term retail. You either knew a broker, had connections, or spent hours cold-calling leasing offices.

## The Solution: A Name That Works

"Rentail Space" = _rental_ + _retail_. Completely ownable. Strategic brand architecture:

- **Search Dominance**: Google "[rentail space](https://www.google.com/search?q=rentail+space)"—we own page one
- **Instant Clarity**: Name tells you exactly what we do
- **Memorable**: Easy to say, spell, remember

## What We Actually Do

One AI-powered platform shows available kiosks, pop-ups, and inline spaces at shopping centers—with photos, pricing, and foot traffic data. No broker calls needed.

Tell the AI what you're selling, your budget, your timeline. It finds shopping centers near you and ranks them by fit. Direct contact info for every property.
