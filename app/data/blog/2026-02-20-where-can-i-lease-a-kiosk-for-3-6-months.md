## What 3-6 Month Leases Cost

Short-term kiosk leases cost $1,500-$8,000 monthly depending on mall quality and location within the center.

**By mall tier**:

- Community/regional malls: $1,500-$3,000/month
- Mid-tier malls: $2,500-$5,000/month
- Premium properties (Westfield, Brookfield): $4,000-$8,000/month

**Location matters**:

- Prime placement (near entrances, food court): ~$23/sq ft
- Secondary spots (corners, walls): ~$11/sq ft
- Holiday premium: 20-40% increase November-December

**Additional costs**:

- CAM fees: ~$200-$500/month
- Percentage rent (sometimes): 5-10% of gross sales above threshold
- Upfront deposits: First month + security deposit

## What to Tell the AI

**Product category**: "Wireless phone accessories" or "custom jewelry" or "gourmet food samples"—specific terms help match you to appropriate demographics.

**Budget range**: "Up to $3,000 per month" helps exclude ultra-premium malls and focus on centers you can afford.

**Exact duration**: "3-6 months starting in March" differs from "90-day trial with option to extend." Some malls prefer 6-month minimums. Others accommodate 3-month trials.

**Target customer**: "College students and young professionals" points to different malls than "affluent families spending $200+ per trip."

## Why 3-6 Months Works

**Seasonal products**: Concentrate selling during peak demand. Don't pay rent in off-seasons.

**Product testing**: Validate demand with real customers before committing long-term. Results from 3 months of actual sales beat any focus group.

**Brand building**: Pop-up launches build awareness without permanent retail infrastructure costs. If it works, negotiate a longer-term inline space.

**Geographic expansion**: Test new markets with limited commitment. Learn local preferences before signing expensive long-term leases.

## Types of Kiosk Spaces

**RMUs (Retail Merchandising Units)**: ~50 square feet. Mobile carts you operate around. Ideal for simple displays, jewelry, accessories. Lowest cost.

**Standard kiosks**: 100-200 square feet. Enclosed structures you operate from inside. Good for products requiring demos, cash wraps, inventory storage.

**Temporary inline spaces**: 400-1,200 square feet. Actual storefronts for short-term leases when malls have vacancies. More setup required but full retail experience.
