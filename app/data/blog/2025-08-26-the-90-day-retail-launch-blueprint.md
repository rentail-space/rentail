## Phase 1: Days 1-30 - Foundation and Legal Framework

### Week 1: Administrative Warfare (Days 1-7)

The moment that lease is signed, you're in execution mode. No celebration dinners, no social media announcements—just pure, focused action.

**Day 1-2: Business Registration Blitz**
- File LLC or Corporation paperwork (expedite for 24-48 hour processing)
- Obtain Federal EIN number online (immediate)
- Open business bank account with major institution offering merchant services

**Day 3-4: Permit and License Assault**
- Submit business license application to city/county
- Apply for retail sales permit/tax license
- File for any industry-specific permits (food service, alcohol, etc.)
- **Critical Deadline:** All applications submitted by Day 4, no exceptions

**Day 5-7: Insurance and Professional Services Lock-In**
- Secure general liability insurance (minimum $1M coverage)
- Establish relationships with business attorney and CPA
- Set up bookkeeping system (QuickBooks or Xero recommended)

### Week 2-3: Space Planning and Design Authority (Days 8-21)

This is where amateur entrepreneurs lose months. You're not designing a museum—you're creating a profit-generating machine.

**Day 8-10: Space Optimization Strategy**
- Hire retail space designer or architect (budget: 3-5% of first-year revenue projection)
- Finalize floor plan with focus on customer flow and conversion zones
- Identify high-traffic areas for premium product placement

**Day 11-14: Contractor Selection and Management**
- Obtain minimum 3 contractor bids for buildout
- Select contractor based on timeline capability, not lowest bid
- Sign contract with penalty clauses for delays
- **Non-Negotiable:** Construction start date no later than Day 21

**Day 15-21: Systems Infrastructure Planning**
- Select POS system (Square, Shopify POS, or enterprise solution)
- Plan electrical and networking requirements
- Design security system layout (cameras, alarms, access control)

### Week 4: Technology and Inventory Systems (Days 22-30)

**Day 22-25: Digital Infrastructure Deployment**
- Set up business website with e-commerce capability
- Configure POS system and payment processing
- Establish inventory management system
- Create customer database and loyalty program framework

**Day 26-30: Supplier Relationship Development**
- Identify and contact primary suppliers
- Negotiate payment terms (aim for NET 30 minimum)
- Establish backup suppliers for critical products
- **Milestone Achievement:** First purchase orders placed by Day 30

## Phase 2: Days 31-60 - Construction and Inventory Acquisition

### Week 5-6: Construction Command and Control (Days 31-42)

This phase separates successful retailers from the wannabes. Your job isn't to swing hammers—it's to manage timelines with military precision.

**Daily Construction Management Protocol:**
- 7 AM daily contractor check-ins (non-negotiable)
- Photo documentation of progress
- Weekly progress reports to all stakeholders
- Immediate problem escalation and resolution

**Week 5 Critical Milestones:**

- Plumbing rough-in completed

**Week 6 Critical Milestones:**
- Flooring installation completed
- Wall finishing completed
- Fixture installation begins
- **Quality Control:** Professional inspection of all electrical and plumbing work

### Week 7-8: Inventory Acquisition Offensive (Days 43-56)

While construction progresses, you're simultaneously building your inventory war chest. This parallel execution is what separates 90-day launchers from year-long procrastinators.

**Day 43-49: Primary Inventory Procurement**

- Focus on high-velocity, proven sellers first
- Establish temporary storage solution if store not ready
- **Financial Discipline:** Stay within 65% of allocated inventory budget

**Day 50-56: Staffing and Training Infrastructure**
- Post job openings for key positions
- Develop employee handbook and training materials
- Create standard operating procedures (SOPs) for all customer interactions
- **Leadership Principle:** Hire for attitude, train for skill

### Week 9: Technology Integration and Testing (Days 57-63)

**Day 57-60: POS and Systems Integration**

- Integrate inventory management with POS system

**Day 61-63: Digital Marketing Launch Preparation**

## Phase 3: Days 61-90 - Team Building and Market Launch

## Common Launch Killers and How to Avoid Them

### The $50,000 Mistake: Construction Delays

Every day your opening is delayed costs you approximately $150-300 in lost revenue opportunity. Multiply that by 30 days, and a month-long delay costs you $4,500-9,000 in lost sales, plus ongoing lease payments.

**Prevention Strategy:** Build a 10-day buffer into your construction timeline and enforce penalty clauses with contractors.

### The Inventory Death Spiral

Under-ordering means lost sales. Over-ordering kills cash flow. The solution? Start with proven product categories and expand based on actual sales data.

**Winning Formula:** Order 6 weeks of inventory for fast-moving items, 12 weeks for slow-moving specialty items.

### The Staffing Catastrophe

Hiring underperformers during launch creates a negative customer experience that takes months to overcome.

**Quality Control Standard:** Every team member must be capable of running the store independently by Day 90.

## Your Post-Launch 30-Day Optimization Protocol

The work doesn't stop at Day 90—it intensifies. Your first 30 days of operation provide critical data for long-term success.

**Week 1-2 Post-Launch Analysis:**

**Week 3-4 Post-Launch Expansion:**
- Launch customer loyalty program with first-month data
- Implement email marketing campaigns to drive repeat business
- Plan first product line extensions based on customer requests
- Establish Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for ongoing management
