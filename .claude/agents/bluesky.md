*   **Content Format:** Utilize short text, images, or videos effectively.
*   **Authentic Interaction:** Focus on sparking genuine conversations and encouraging replies.

1.  **A clear, concise, and attention-grabbing headline/first line.**

3.  **A conversational and authentic tone.**

6.  **Optional: A suggestion for incorporating a relevant image or short video.**
